# Cleaning Support — early user testing

This is an early development build. It is intended to learn what feels helpful
or difficult, not to measure productivity or consistency.

## Before you begin

- Android phone or emulator running Android 6.0 or newer.
- The app currently stores data locally on the device.
- This build is not a final release. Keep a backup if you enter anything you
  want to keep.

## Suggested session

Please use the app for 10–15 minutes and talk through what you expect to happen.
You do not need to finish every activity.

1. Create or choose a starter goal for a room that matters to you.
2. Open Today and identify the first small step.
3. Complete a step, then try Undo or mark it as not done.
4. Try the energy-based rescue mode with the energy you have right now.
5. Skip a step for now and notice how the message feels.
6. Open History and select a day on the monthly calendar.
7. Open Settings and try the text-size slider and backup export.

## Feedback questions

- Where did you feel unsure what to do next?
- Did any wording feel judgmental, demanding, or confusing?
- Did the suggested step feel small enough to begin?
- Which controls felt useful when your energy was low?
- Were any screens too busy, too small, or difficult to navigate?
- What would make you comfortable opening the app again tomorrow?

## Safety and privacy

The app is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat ADHD. Do not enter
private information that is not needed for testing. To remove test data, use
the device's app-data controls. Synchronization is intentionally deferred.
